• SFTP: a built-in editor for the files on your server — text files are edited in place and keep their encoding, line endings and permissions, configuration files are syntax coloured, and anything that is not text opens in a hex editor
• SSH keys: a key with a passphrase now works — the passphrase is saved with the key, encrypted by the device when that option is on. Keys imported earlier must be imported again; the list marks them.
